A PRACTICAL PATH FORWARD

From useful demo to trusted platform.

A staged plan to prove value with shop owners, protect their data, and grow only where customer needs point us.

01

Meeting MVP IN THIS BUILD

Demonstrate the core value loop with a local sample shop.

  • Catalog, sale entry, refunds, responsive dashboard
  • Live calculations and browser persistence
  • Clearly positioned as concept validation, not production SaaS
02

API, identity & tenancy

Go modular monolith, PostgreSQL, migrations, secure sessions, organization membership and tenant-scoped authorization.

03

Catalog & reliable transactions

Server validated products, historical sale snapshots, idempotent writes, atomic sales and partial refunds with over-refund protection.

04

Analytics & onboarding

Timezone-aware date filters, shared metric definitions, comparisons, onboarding, exports, and user-validated reports.

05

Teams, plans & operations

Role-based permissions, expiring invitations, billing entitlements, audit logs, recovery, backups and observability.

06

Imports & advanced intelligence

CSV/POS integrations first; then branches, inventory, margin, forecasts and explainable opt-in insights as demand is proven.
